💡 Tips for Success

  • • Be authentic - share genuine experiences and reflections
  • • Look at the camera - not at yourself on screen
  • • Sit upright with good posture
  • • Use specific examples and personal stories
  • • Professional setting - clean background, good lighting
  • • Dress professionally as you would for a real interview

⚠️ Important Notes

  • • Analysis takes 30-60 seconds after completing all questions
  • • Answer length is flexible - a concise 1-minute answer can score as highly as a detailed 3-minute response
  • • You cannot pause or restart once you begin
  • • Exiting early will not save progress or return your credit
  • • Make sure you have a stable internet connection before starting - your videos need to upload for analysis
  • • Do not refresh or close this page during the interview - this will end your session early
  • • Feedback is constructive and designed to help you improve
